# Contributor Code of Conduct

## 1. Professional Conduct & Core Purpose
DeepFake-AI is an open-source scientific research and engineering benchmark dedicated to synthetic media forensics, digital authenticity verification, and explainable artificial intelligence (XAI).

All participants, contributors, and maintainers are expected to maintain professional standards of conduct, collaborative integrity, and mutual respect.

## 2. Universal Standards of Collaboration
We expect all collaborators and community members to uphold the following principles:

* **Focus on Technical Merit**: Direct all discussions, code reviews, and technical debates toward architecture, algorithmic accuracy, reproducibility, data integrity, and code quality.
* **Mutual Respect**: Treat every contributor, reviewer, and community member with courtesy, professionalism, and patience, regardless of their background, seniority, or experience level.
* **Constructive Communication**: Offer actionable, objective, and polite feedback on pull requests and issues. Receive technical critique with an open, constructive mindset.
* **Academic & Research Integrity**: Maintain rigorous standards of truthfulness in benchmark results, prevent dataset identity leakage, accurately cite and attribute prior research, and respect software licenses and intellectual property.
* **Zero Harassment**: Disrespectful conduct, personal attacks, trolling, derogatory remarks, abusive language, or harassment of any kind will not be tolerated.

## 3. Project Scope
These standards apply to all project repositories, issue trackers, pull request discussions, documentation, and communications representing the DeepFake-AI project.

## 4. Enforcement & Reporting
Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and upholding these standards. Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported directly to the lead maintainer at `darrehanrasool@gmail.com`. All reports will be reviewed promptly, objectively, and confidentially.
