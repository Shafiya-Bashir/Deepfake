# Invention Disclosure Form: Multi-Domain Media Forensics Architecture

## 1. Title of Invention
SYSTEM AND METHOD FOR MULTI-DOMAIN DEEPFAKE DETECTION VIA COUPLED SPATIAL-SPECTRAL CROSS-ATTENTION AND TEMPORAL PHYSIOLOGICAL CONSISTENCY

## 3. Technical Field
The present invention relates generally to digital media forensics, computer vision, artificial intelligence, and cybersecurity, and more particularly to systems and methods for detecting synthetic facial manipulations and AI-generated media.

## 4. Problem Solved
Existing deepfake detectors rely on spatial boundaries or frequency spectra in isolation. Spatial detectors fail on compressed video, while frequency detectors fail on modern latent diffusion models. The proposed invention solves this vulnerability by adaptively fusing spatial boundary confidence with high-frequency 2D-DCT residuals using cross-attention, validated against spatio-temporal physiological cues.
