# FakeAVCeleb Multimodal Dataset Specification

## Overview
- **Paper**: FakeAVCeleb: A Novel Audio-Video Multimodal Deepfake Dataset (Khalid et al., NeurIPS 2022)
- **Modalities**:
  - Video Deepfake: Face swapping (Faceswap, FSGAN)
  - Audio Deepfake: Voice cloning (SV2TTS, Wav2Lip)
  - Multimodal Deepfake: Both face and voice synthesized
  - Desynchronized Authentic: Real audio paired with mismatched real video
