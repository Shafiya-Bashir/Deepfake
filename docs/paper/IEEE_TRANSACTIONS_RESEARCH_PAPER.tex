\begin{abstract}
The rapid proliferation of deep generative models has enabled the synthesis of highly convincing human face imagery and videos. While single-domain detectors achieve high in-domain accuracy, their cross-generator generalization drops sharply under lossy compression and novel synthesis techniques. This paper introduces DeepFake-AI, an end-to-end framework integrating spatial boundary inspection, 2D Discrete Cosine Transform (DCT) high-pass spectral decomposition, and spatio-temporal physiological consistency. Across extensive evaluations on FaceForensics++, Celeb-DF v2, DFDC, FakeAVCeleb, and GenImage, DeepFake-AI achieves 99.6\% AUC on FaceForensics++ and 87.5\% zero-shot cross-dataset AUC on Celeb-DF v2, outperforming standalone baselines.
\end{abstract}
