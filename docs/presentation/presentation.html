1. Threat Evolution & Generative Landscape

  • Phase 1 (2018-2020): Autoencoders & Early GANs (DeepFaceLab, ProGAN, CycleGAN) leaving visible boundary seams.
  • Phase 2 (2020-2023): Style-based synthesis (StyleGAN2/3) and video reenactment (FOMM, SadTalker).
  • Phase 3 (2023-2026): Latent diffusion models (Stable Diffusion, Midjourney, GenImage) bypassing standard GAN frequency artifacts.

2. Dual-Stream Spatial-Frequency Architecture

  • Spatial Stream: ResNet & Xception capturing spatial texture & jawline blending boundary discontinuities.
  • Frequency Stream: 2D-DCT octave high-pass filtering capturing deconvolution and upsampling periodicities.
  • Cross-Attention Fusion: Dynamically focuses on spectral anomalies where spatial features are ambiguous.
