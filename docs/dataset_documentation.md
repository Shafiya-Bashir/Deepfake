# Dataset Documentation: Deepfake Detection Benchmarks

This document outlines the benchmark datasets integrated and supported by DeepFake-AI.

## 1. FaceForensics++ (FF++)
- **Authors**: Rossler et al. (ICCV 2019)
- **Institution**: Technical University of Munich (TUM), Friedrich-Alexander-Universität (FAU)
- **Scale**: 1,000 pristine original YouTube videos, 5,000 synthetic videos across 5 manipulation methods:
  - **Deepfakes**: Autoencoder-based face swapping.
  - **Face2Face**: Facial reenactment transferring source facial expressions.
  - **FaceSwap**: Graphics-based 3D landmark mesh fitting and color blending.
  - **NeuralTextures**: Neural rendering pipeline modifying mouth and interior facial expressions.
  - **FaceShifter**: High-fidelity face swapping handling occlusions and pose variations.
- **Compression Variants**:
  - `raw`: Uncompressed ProRes.
  - `c23`: High quality (H.264 constant rate factor 23).
  - `c40`: Heavy compression (H.264 constant rate factor 40).

## 2. Celeb-DF (v2)
- **Authors**: Li et al. (CVPR 2020)
- **Institution**: SUNY Albany
- **Scale**: 590 real YouTube celebrity clips, 5,639 high-quality deepfake face swaps.
- **Significance**: Addresses low visual quality and obvious boundary artifacts present in early datasets. Celeb-DF serves as the gold standard for testing cross-dataset generalization.

## 3. Deepfake Detection Challenge (DFDC)
- **Authors**: Dolhansky et al. (2020)
- **Institution**: Meta AI, AWS, Microsoft, Partnership on AI
- **Scale**: Over 124,000 videos featuring 3,426 paid actors recorded across diverse lighting, backgrounds, camera qualities, and angles.
- **Significance**: Captures extreme real-world variability and in-the-wild video artifacts.

## 4. FakeAVCeleb
- **Authors**: Khalid et al. (NeurIPS 2022)
- **Institution**: KAIST, Sungkyunkwan University
- **Scale**: 21,566 video clips across 500 celebrities spanning diverse ethnic backgrounds.
- **Significance**: Unifies video manipulation (Faceswap, FSGAN) and synthetic audio cloning (SV2TTS, Wav2Lip) to benchmark multimodal lip-sync and voice consistency.

## 5. GenImage
- **Authors**: Zhu et al. (NeurIPS 2024)
- **Institution**: Chinese Academy of Sciences
- **Scale**: 1.3 million images generated by 8 advanced generative engines (Stable Diffusion v1.4/1.5, Midjourney, DALL-E 3, ADM, Glide, VQ-GAN) paired with real ImageNet classes.
- **Significance**: Benchmark specifically assessing detection of modern diffusion-synthesized images.
