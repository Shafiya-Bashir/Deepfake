# Model Card: ST2-SyncNet (Spatial-Temporal-Spectral Synchronization Network)

### Model Details
- **Model Type**: Tri-Modal Neural Forensic Architecture
- **Authors**: Dar Rehan Rasool, Dr. Riyaz, Dr. Javad
- **Affiliation**: Islamic University of Science and Technology (IUST), Kashmir
- **License**: MIT

### Intended Use
- Verification of facial video and image authenticity against deepfake tampering, GAN generation, and diffusion synthesis.

### Metrics & Performance
- FaceForensics++ (c23): 99.62% AUC
- Celeb-DF v2: 87.50% AUC (Zero-shot generalization)
- DFDC: 79.40% AUC
